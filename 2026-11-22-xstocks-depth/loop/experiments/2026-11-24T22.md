## What it says for the gate: not settled, leaning go
- Measured: the ranking holds on day two. COINx (50.1 on the 24th), CRCLx 30.4, MSTRx 45.3 stay above 20; NVDAx stays
  the lowest at $10k (10.0 on the 24th, 9.1 median over both days). Every hedgeable name has a best-side $10k room of 10 bp or
  more in the Tuesday rows (lowest: TSLAx 11.1, GOOGLx 12.9, MSFTx 12.9, NVDAx 10.0).
- Measured change: METAx fell from 26.0 (Monday) to 18.4 (Tuesday); GMEx from 29.3 to 20.3. Two days is too few to call a trend.
- Caveat on method: the Tuesday figures above are max of the two per-side medians from `--by day`, not the median of the
  per-hour max that `open.py` reports; they can differ slightly.
- Stop test, counter-case sought ("every name under 10 bp at $10k, open"): smallest open best-side median is NVDAx 9.1; the
  next is MSFTx 13.0, and 8 more names/rows exceed 13; not found. Second clause (room only where hedge is `-`): not met.
  Stop is not met.
- Why not go: 15 open hours, two sessions, under 24 hours in the session; the gate wants a week with five US sessions
  (we have 2). Volume per name ("hundreds of thousands a day") is not in the table: not computed. Room at $10k is
  tens of bp on one side on COINx, CRCLx, GMEx, MSTRx, METAx (median >= 21.1), but 5 names sit at 13-16 bp.
- Inferred, not measured: the new wide names (AVGOx 145, HOODx 156, INTCx 311) are likely thin or stale Solana pools, so
  room there is probably not a fillable edge; they have 7 hours each.
